## Medical clearance before flying
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_HK/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/medical-assistance/passenger-medical-clearance-form-and-medical-card.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: do I need a doctor's letter to fly; medical form; fit to fly; medical clearance; MEDIF; MEDA form; I have a heart condition

For some medical conditions, Cathay asks for a medical clearance form filled in by your doctor.
The form should reach Cathay as early as possible, and no later than 48 hours before your flight.

## Breathing machines and other medical devices
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_HK/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/medical-assistance/medical-devices.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I use my CPAP on the plane; sleep apnea machine; breathing machine; ventilator; can I plug in my medical device

You can use your own breathing machine on board. It does not need medical clearance, but tell Cathay at least 48 hours before your flight.
Medical devices used on board must run on their own batteries.

## Oxygen on the flight
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/medical-assistance/medical-oxygen.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: I need oxygen on the plane; can I bring my oxygen tank; oxygen cylinder; portable oxygen concentrator; oxygen bottle

Cathay can provide medical oxygen on board if it is arranged in advance. The forms should be sent in 72 hours before the flight.
You cannot bring your own oxygen cylinders. An approved portable oxygen concentrator is allowed, with a statement from your doctor
and enough charged batteries for one and a half times the flight.

## Medication on the flight
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/medical-assistance/medications-and-needles.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can the crew keep my medicine in the fridge; insulin; injections on the plane; can the crew give me my injection; needles; my pills

The cabin crew cannot keep medication in a fridge, and they cannot give injections or medication.
Bring what you need for the flight, and make sure you or the person travelling with you can give it.
