## Checked baggage
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/faqs/baggage/check-in-baggage-allowance/what-is-my-free-checked-baggage-allowance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: how many bags can I bring; luggage allowance; how many suitcases; how heavy can my bag be; baggage limit; checked bag weight

It depends on your fare. In Economy, the Light fare includes one checked bag of up to 23 kilograms,
and the Essential and Flex fares include two bags of up to 23 kilograms each.
Your own allowance is printed on your e-ticket.

## Cabin baggage
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/faqs/baggage/cabin-baggage-allowance/what-is-my-carry-on-baggage-allowance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: hand luggage; carry-on bag; what can I take on the plane with me; cabin bag weight; hand baggage

In Economy you can take one cabin bag of up to 7 kilograms.
You can also carry small necessary items, such as a walking stick.

## Check-in times
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/destinations/airports/hong-kong-international-airport.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: how early should I arrive at the airport; what time does check-in close; when does check-in open; when should I get to the airport; how long before my flight

Check-in counters normally close 60 minutes before departure. At Hong Kong, the counters open three hours before departure.
Times are different at some airports, so check the email Cathay sends you before the flight.

## Special meals
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/faqs/manage-your-booking/request-special-assistance/can-i-request-special-meals-on-my-flight.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I order a special meal; vegetarian meal; diabetic meal; soft food; food allergy; change my meal

Cathay has more than twenty special meals. Order yours at least 24 hours before the flight.
If you have a food allergy, tell Cathay at least three days before.

## Changing or cancelling a ticket
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/book-a-trip/change-cancel-refund.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I change my flight; change the date; cancel my ticket; refund; is there a change fee; what if I cannot travel

It depends on your fare. With an Economy Flex fare, changing your flight is free apart from any difference in the fare.
With the Light and Essential fares there is a fee as well. The exact amounts are in the fare rules of your ticket.
