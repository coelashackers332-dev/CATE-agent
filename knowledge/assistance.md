## Wheelchair help at the airport
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/mobility-assistance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: who pushes my wheelchair; help getting to the gate; someone to take me through the airport; do you have wheelchairs; does the wheelchair cost extra; is wheelchair help free

Cathay provides a wheelchair and an attendant for passengers who need help getting around.
The attendant takes you through the airport to the gate, or to the aircraft door where the airport allows.
There is no extra charge for this.

## Asking for wheelchair help
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: how do I request a wheelchair; how far ahead do I need to ask for assistance; when should I tell you I need help; is it too late to ask for a wheelchair

Tell Cathay as early as you can, ideally when you book, and at least two to three days before the flight.
If you ask later than that, staff will still try their best to help you on the day.

## Bringing your own wheelchair
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/mobility-assistance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I take my own wheelchair on the plane; can I bring my wheelchair; does my wheelchair count as baggage; is there a charge for my wheelchair; where does my wheelchair go

You can bring your own wheelchair. Cathay carries it free of charge, on top of your baggage allowance.
You can use it up to the departure gate, and to the aircraft door where the airport allows.
Cathay needs its weight, its size and its battery type before you fly.

## Battery-powered wheelchairs and scooters
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_TW/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/mobility-assistance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: electric wheelchair; mobility scooter; lithium battery in my wheelchair; spare battery for my wheelchair; power chair

Battery-powered wheelchairs are accepted. A lithium battery can stay in the chair if it is securely attached and protected.
A battery that is removed, or a spare, travels with you in the cabin: one spare of up to 300 watt-hours, or two of up to 160 watt-hours each.
Tell Cathay the battery type and its watt-hours before you fly.

## Walking sticks, crutches and walkers
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/elderly-passengers.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I bring my walking stick; cane on the plane; crutches; walking frame; rollator; does my walker count as baggage

You can bring a walking stick, crutches or a walker on board, as long as it can be stowed safely.
Mobility aids are carried in addition to your baggage allowance.

## Help from the cabin crew
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/mobility-assistance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: will the crew help me to my seat; help getting to the toilet on the plane; is there a wheelchair on the plane; onboard wheelchair; aisle chair

The cabin crew can help you move to and from your seat.
There is a wheelchair on board, and the crew can take you in it as far as the lavatory door.

## What the cabin crew cannot do
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/mobility-assistance.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can the crew help me eat; can the crew give me my medicine; help inside the toilet; personal care on the flight; feeding

The cabin crew cannot help with personal care. That means eating and drinking, taking medication, and help inside the lavatory.
If you need that kind of help, Cathay asks you to travel with someone who can give it.

## Travelling alone or with a companion
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/safety-assistants.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I fly alone; do I need someone to travel with me; do I need a companion; safety assistant; can I travel by myself in a wheelchair; escort

Most passengers with a disability can travel on their own.
You must travel with a safety assistant if you could not help in your own evacuation in an emergency, or could not follow safety instructions.
The assistant can be a relative or someone you hire.

## Seats for passengers who need assistance
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/seating-and-accessibility.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I get an aisle seat; seat with armrest that lifts; can I sit by the exit; seat near the toilet; which seat is best for a wheelchair user

Cathay tries to give you a seat that suits your needs. There are aisle seats with armrests that lift up.
For safety, passengers who need help moving cannot sit in an emergency exit row.

## Accessible lavatory on board
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/seating-and-accessibility.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: is there a disabled toilet on the plane; accessible toilet; a toilet I can use in a wheelchair; wheelchair toilet; bathroom for wheelchair users; grab bar

Every Cathay aircraft has a lavatory with a wider door and an extra grab bar.
The crew can help you to the door, but not inside.

## Check-in help for older passengers
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/elderly-passengers.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: is there a special counter for elderly passengers; help at check-in; where do I check in at Hong Kong airport; senior counter

At Hong Kong International Airport there is a special check-in counter in Area C of the check-in hall.
At other airports, the ground staff at the check-in counter can help you.

## Passengers with sight loss
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/visually-impaired-passengers.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: I am blind; I cannot see well; visually impaired; help for blind passengers; someone to guide me; white cane

Cathay can escort you to and from the aircraft, give you your own safety briefing, and let you board first.
The crew can read the menu to you and tell you where the food is on your tray. Your cane travels free of charge.

## Passengers with hearing loss
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/hearing-speech-impaired-passengers.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: I am deaf; hard of hearing; hearing aid on the plane; I cannot hear announcements; hearing impaired

Cathay can escort you to and from the aircraft and give you your own safety briefing. The safety video has subtitles.
You can bring your hearing aid without telling Cathay in advance.

## Guide dogs and assistance dogs
Source: https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/prepare-trip/help-for-passengers/disability-and-mobility-assistance/assistance-dogs.html
Checked: 2026-11-14 draft
Asks: can I bring my guide dog; assistance dog in the cabin; service dog; does my guide dog fly free

An accredited guide dog or assistance dog can travel with you in the cabin free of charge.
Bring the dog's original documents. The rules depend on the country, and Hong Kong needs a special permit.
Tell Cathay at least two days before the flight.
This is only for trained assistance dogs. I do not have information about pets.
